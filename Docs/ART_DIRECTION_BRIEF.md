# Blood Directive - Art Direction Brief

## Visual Identity
Dark, grounded, classified underground action RPG. Modern concrete and steel contaminated by alien breach effects and forbidden Aether science.

## Canon Source and Scope
This brief defines the enduring visual rules. `ARTWORK_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` translates them into character, environment, HUD, and delivery requirements. The approved Act 1 sequence in `ACT1_STORY_AND_TEN_LEVEL_MAP_BIBLE.md` is the authority for level order, story reveals, alien presence, and gameplay landmarks.

Older eight-sector bunker documents remain useful macro-layout references, but their earlier Containment Hive start must not replace the approved Level 01 Obsidian Briefing Vault opening.

## Core Mood
Classified. Underground. Hostile. Occult. Militarized. Ancient. Contained until it fails.

## Reference Feel
Reference for mood and composition only; do not copy assets.

- Diablo 2: readable isometric darkness, compact spaces, strong silhouettes.
- Path of Exile 2: grounded material detail, dark brutal tone.
- Underground facility fiction: reinforced concrete corridors, blast doors, industrial lights.
- Containment fiction: classified experiments, warning lights, failing systems.

dark desaturated grounded isometric action RPG, classified underground bunker, worn concrete, aged steel, industrial fluorescent lighting, red warning lights, tactical occult sci-fi, realistic game asset, readable silhouette, no logos, no readable text, no flags, no real unit identifiers

## Character Style
Grounded and physically plausible. No superhero armor, no oversized sci-fi plating, no real-world unit patches.

Phantom Army operative direction:
- lean, wiry survivalist body
- rugged veteran face
- dark olive/black/gunmetal clothing
- compact armor plates
- pouches, gloves, boots
- small Aether device accents

## Grey Enemy Style
Short Grey:
- thin body
- large head
- black eyes
- pale grey skin
- readable from isometric view

Tall Grey:
- taller, threatening, elongated limbs
- faint Aether glow
- more deliberate posture

## Reptilian Enemy Style
- The lower-bunker invasive force: dense, powerful, non-human silhouettes with controlled, predatory movement.
- Earthy black, oxidized green-grey, and restrained Aether contamination distinguish them from Grey intervention equipment.
- Use breach damage, displaced containment hardware, and fear-pressure effects to show their origin beneath the facility.
- Do not give them Grey biometric lamps, medical white plating, or sanctioned-quarantine markings.

## Species Boundaries
- Greys are the first active enemy force in the playable slice. Short Greys are scouts, technicians, containment units, and swarm security; Tall Greys are coordinators and intervention controllers.
- Reptilians are strongest in the lower Act 1 levels and originate from the buried Aether fault, not the public entrance.
- Nordics are ambiguous later observers, not friendly helpers or a routine Act 1 enemy.
- Insectoids are a later, terrifying non-human force and must not be silently substituted for Greys or Reptilians.
- The arriving hostile extraterrestrial power is intentionally unnamed. Do not invent a creature, emblem, or faction look for it before the story names it.

## Environment Style
Act 1 must feel indoors and underground.

Required motifs:
- reinforced concrete walls
- steel blast doors
- overhead pipes
- cable trays
- fluorescent strip lights
- red warning lights
- grated drains
- observation windows
- containment doors
- thick structural pillars

Avoid:
- outdoor skybox feeling
- spaceship panels
- giant empty square rooms
- bright neon cyberpunk look
- fantasy dungeon stone for Act 1

## UI Style
Dark classified bunker-tech. Compact, readable, minimal decoration. Red/cyan/amber accents.

## HUD Meaning
- Vitality uses urgent red only for damage and critical states.
- Aether is cyan, visibly changes at collection, and supports the calibrated weapon state.
- Weapon readout distinguishes Standard from Aether Calibrated without relying on color alone.
- Objective and contextual prompts are short, state-driven, and do not pre-empt story revelations.
- Enemy health bars are reserved for active threats and disappear on defeat.
