# Blood Directive - Game Design Bible

## Elevator Pitch
Blood Directive is a dark isometric action RPG where an off-record black-file operative is recalled to a buried Cold War-era bunker during a hostile Grey alien breach. The player survives sealed underground corridors, extracts a scientist, collects Aether, and uncovers a hidden war involving alien factions and human power networks.

## Genre
Isometric action RPG / tactical dungeon crawler.

## Platform
PC first. Steam target later.

## Player Fantasy
A hardened off-record operative moving through classified underground facilities, surviving close-quarters alien contact with disciplined weapons, tactical movement, and experimental Aether upgrades.

## Core Loop
Move through a bunker route -> encounter enemy group -> fight -> collect Aether/loot -> interact with story object or NPC -> unlock the next section -> reach extraction.

## Camera
Fixed isometric ARPG camera inspired by Diablo-style readability. The camera should show enough corridor context without becoming a ceiling view.

## Movement
Left-click movement is foundational. It must be reliable before combat complexity is added.

Required behavior:
- Click visible floor to move.
- Walls and closed doors block movement.
- Character rotates toward movement direction.
- Movement feels immediate and readable.

## Combat
Real-time ARPG combat with small enemy groups. Combat should feel dangerous but readable.

Initial combat target:
- One basic attack.
- One tactical ability.
- Short Grey enemy group.
- One stronger Grey variant.

## Aether
Aether is the unknown energy/resource that powers alien breach phenomena and weapon upgrades. The rescued scientist helps the player understand and weaponize it.

Aether should function as:
- story mystery
- upgrade resource
- damage/resistance theme
- future travel technology justification

## Main Character
The initial playable character is a fictional off-record black-file operative. The tone should be lean, survivalist, tactical, and grounded. Avoid real unit names and identifiers.

## Key NPC
The scientist is the first major story NPC. They understand Aether and become the weapon/research upgrade anchor.

## Alien Species
Greys: first enemy force; drones, Short Greys, Tall Greys, psychic/technical variants.
Reptilians: hostile elite infiltrators and commanders.
Nordics: ambiguous observers, not immediately trusted.
Insectoids: later terrifying nonhuman force.

## Act Plan
Act 1: buried Cold War bunker escape.
Act 2: remote U.S. desert and first base camp.
Act 3: South American jungle investigation.
Act 4: water/Atlantis arc.
Act 5: Antarctica forbidden-zone descent.

## Sequel Setup
Human leadership is compromised by hidden power networks and alien influence. The Cabal-like human faction wants Aether weapons to expand control and fight other alien races. The Annunaki/Watchers become larger sequel-level escalation.
