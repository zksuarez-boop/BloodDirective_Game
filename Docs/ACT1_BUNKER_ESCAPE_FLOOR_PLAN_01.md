# Act 1 Bunker Escape Floor Plan 01

## Purpose
Convert the approved bunker-hive concept into a practical first-pass layout before any Unity blockout begins. This document describes a single continuous 45-minute route across three connected subterranean floors.

## Board Legend
- Cyan route: primary playable route and vertical connections.
- Amber route: optional loop, shortcut, or future reward branch.
- Red gate: story or security gate that controls pacing.
- Lift symbol: elevator, freight lift, or vertical shaft connection.

## Lower Depths: Breach Origin

### 1. Containment Hive
- Start location: player enters in a damaged outer containment ring after the Reptilian breach.
- Shape: circular ring with a central rupture and four cell pods.
- Purpose: establish the breach, teach close-range movement, and show that the lowest level is not normal bunker infrastructure.
- Main exit: east service connector toward the geothermal plant.
- Optional loop: west cell-block route returns to the ring and can later hold a reward or escaped specimen event.

### 2. Service Tunnels and Geothermal Plant
- Shape: narrow pipe corridors widening into a three-reactor industrial arena.
- Purpose: first hostile Grey response group and first multi-enemy movement test.
- Main exit: freight lift to Mid Depths, unlocked after the immediate breach route is cleared.
- Optional loop: lower maintenance crawlspace reconnects to the lift foyer and teaches the player that loops exist.

## Mid Depths: Hidden Operations

### 3. Cargo Logistics Depot
- Arrival point: freight lift from Service and Geothermal.
- Shape: broad cargo floor with stackable cover lanes and loading offices around the perimeter.
- Purpose: rescue the scientist and establish the level's operational heart.
- Main gate: the Aether Relay route remains locked until the scientist is rescued.
- Return shortcut: after Aether collection, a logistics-side corridor provides a quick route back for calibration.

### 4. Aether Relay Cathedral
- Shape: large central octagonal chamber with relay machinery creating line-of-sight breaks.
- Purpose: collect the Aether sample and reveal that Grey intervention protocol is compromised.
- Main exit: northeast research corridor after collection.
- Return route: west/south shortcut drops the player back into Logistics for calibration without repeating the full route.

### 5. Research Laboratories
- Shape: narrow observation halls wrapped around larger experiment rooms.
- Purpose: show what the facility was studying and transition the player from raw breach evidence to command-level knowledge.
- Main exit: secured elevator to Upper Depths, enabled by the scientist's calibration.
- Optional loop: specimen archive and diagnostics bay can become later optional content.

## Upper Depths: Escape and Exposure

### 6. Central Operations Nexus
- Arrival point: secured elevator from Research Laboratories.
- Shape: circular command atrium with four spokes and clear sightlines toward every major exit.
- Purpose: navigation reset, story reveal, and the bridge between classified research and public-facing facility operations.
- Main gate: Intake and Quarantine is now Grey-controlled and must be pushed through to reach evacuation.

### 7. Intake and Quarantine
- Shape: controlled decontamination lanes, security checkpoints, and administrative rooms.
- Purpose: prove the Greys arrived through official channels and turn the familiar entrance infrastructure into a hostile final approach.
- Main exit: final security door leading to the hangar.
- Optional loop: a quarantine bypass may become a high-risk reward path instead of a mandatory route.

### 8. Evacuation Hangar
- Shape: broad aircraft bay with cover, open flanks, a visible extraction craft, and a final pad.
- Purpose: final guard encounter, space for sustained movement, and a visually obvious climax.
- End state: defeat the extraction guard and activate the evacuation pad.

## Critical Route
Containment Hive -> Service and Geothermal -> Cargo Logistics -> Aether Relay -> Logistics calibration shortcut -> Research Laboratories -> Operations Nexus -> Intake and Quarantine -> Evacuation Hangar.

## Playtime Budget
- Lower Depths: 12 minutes.
- Mid Depths: 18 minutes.
- Upper Depths: 15 minutes.

This includes combat, interaction, navigation, short cuts, and a small amount of exploration. Optional loops extend replayability without making the first completion confusing.

## Mapping Boundaries
Eight sector owners can work from this same plan. Shared connections must be reviewed before implementation:

1. Containment Hive connects to Service and Geothermal.
2. Service and Geothermal connects to Cargo Logistics through the freight lift.
3. Cargo Logistics connects to Aether Relay and its calibration shortcut.
4. Aether Relay connects to Research Laboratories and Logistics.
5. Research Laboratories connects to Operations through the secured elevator.
6. Operations connects to Intake and Quarantine.
7. Intake and Quarantine connects to Evacuation Hangar.
8. Evacuation Hangar owns the final extraction state only.
