# Act 1 Story and Ten-Level Map Bible

## Scope
Act 1 is a complete bunker-escape campaign. It begins at the facility's deepest classified level and climbs upward through increasingly public, operational, and compromised layers. It does not enter the desert; the desert forward camp begins Act 2.

Target critical-path playtime is 45 minutes. Optional objectives extend a first run toward 60 minutes without adding mandatory detours.

## High-Level Premise
The player is sent into a classified underground facility on a black operation. Intelligence has confirmed an imminent arrival by an unnamed hostile extraterrestrial power. Every faction already operating on Earth believes the coming arrival will decide who controls the planet's territory, resources, and Aether sites.

The player's mission is to infiltrate the bunker and assassinate high-value alien operatives. The briefing intentionally withholds their identities and the wider political purpose. Before final target authorization arrives, a catastrophic breach begins below the briefing vault.

The operation becomes an escape, rescue, and evidence-recovery mission. The player must get the scientist and an Aether sample out before the bunker is sealed, destroyed, or claimed by another faction.

## Faction Situation

### Reptilians
- Reptilians breach from beneath the facility through a buried Aether fault below the containment complex.
- Their verified population is 833. The exact number is classified intelligence, not public knowledge.
- They feed on the fear frequency created by human panic and use the breach to turn the bunker into a fear-generating pressure chamber.
- Their ranking structure is intentionally deferred for a later lore pass.

### Greys
- Short Greys are ground crews: scouts, technicians, containment units, and swarm-like security teams.
- Tall Greys are coordinators: command personnel, researchers, and intervention-protocol controllers.
- Greys enter through the sanctioned Intake and Quarantine systems in response to the breach.
- They initially appear to be an emergency force opposing the Reptilians. Their protocol has been altered, causing them to classify the player, scientist, and Aether work as containment threats.

### Hidden Human Actor
- An unknown human command layer manipulated the original assassination order and the Grey intervention protocol.
- Its immediate objective is to make alien factions fight over Aether while it collects evidence, leverage, and weapon potential.
- The Act 1 evidence points toward this actor but does not yet reveal the wider human conspiracy planned for the sequel.

## Aether Role
Aether is the medium connecting the underground breach, alien territorial conflict, the coming hostile arrival, and weapon progression. The scientist can calibrate the player's weapons using recovered Aether, turning the escape into the first step of a longer upgrade journey.

## Campaign Flow
The player starts with a false understanding: eliminate alien targets. The player exits with a new understanding: the original mission was engineered, the bunker was studying an Aether fault, the Greys were redirected, and a larger conflict is already forming around Earth.

## Ten-Level Campaign

### 01. Obsidian Briefing Vault
- Depth: deepest classified sublevel.
- Main mission: receive the black-operation briefing and survive the first breach.
- Story beat: target identities are withheld; the briefing is interrupted before authorization completes.
- Layout: secure command room, observation gallery, emergency descent shaft, and one collapsed escape route.
- Optional objective: recover the damaged briefing cipher for a later clue about who issued the order.
- Gate: the breach destroys the planned infiltration route and forces escape into Containment Hive.
- Target time: 3 minutes.

### 02. Containment Hive
- Depth: lowest active containment level.
- Main mission: escape the breached holding ring.
- Story beat: Reptilians entered through the Aether fault beneath the facility, not through the bunker entrance.
- Layout: circular containment ring, central rupture, four cell pods, and an optional survivor corridor.
- Optional objective: rescue a trapped technician or recover a containment specimen record.
- Gate: emergency pressure lock opens only after the immediate breach route is stabilized.
- Target time: 6 minutes.

### 03. Geothermal Undercroft
- Depth: lower industrial level.
- Main mission: restore emergency lift power and climb toward Logistics.
- Story beat: the breach is amplifying fear frequency through the bunker systems.
- Layout: tight service tunnels leading into a three-reactor industrial arena, with a maintenance loop around the reactor floor.
- Optional objective: vent a reactor line to open a future return shortcut.
- Gate: freight lift requires restored auxiliary power.
- Target time: 5 minutes.

### 04. Cargo Logistics Depot
- Depth: mid-level operations.
- Main mission: rescue the scientist.
- Story beat: the scientist identifies the breach energy as Aether and explains why the bunker was studying it.
- Layout: open cargo floor, loading offices, stacked cover lanes, and a secure scientist holding area.
- Optional objective: recover Aether-safe transport cases for an early resource reward.
- Gate: the scientist's access credentials unlock the route toward the Aether Relay.
- Target time: 6 minutes.

### 05. Aether Relay Cathedral
- Depth: mid-level research core.
- Main mission: recover the first live Aether sample.
- Story beat: the sample proves the Reptilians came for research the bunker already possessed; the Greys' containment protocol flags the player as hostile.
- Layout: octagonal relay arena, tall machinery, outer maintenance ring, and a direct but locked return route.
- Optional objective: stabilize relay nodes for additional Aether and a faster return path.
- Gate: the sample activates the Logistics calibration route.
- Target time: 6 minutes.

### 06. Bio-Research Laboratories
- Depth: upper mid-level research.
- Main mission: reach the secured elevator toward Central Operations.
- Story beat: research records show the Grey intervention protocol was changed before the breach.
- Layout: observation corridors, experiment rooms, specimen archive, diagnostics bay, and controlled line-of-sight encounters.
- Optional objective: retrieve a Tall Grey protocol fragment that clarifies the false classification.
- Gate: scientist calibration enables the laboratory elevator.
- Target time: 4 minutes.

### 07. Central Operations Nexus
- Depth: upper administrative level.
- Main mission: restore internal routing and identify a route to evacuation.
- Story beat: bunker leadership abandoned the lower levels while preserving classified Aether research.
- Layout: circular command atrium, vertical lifts, four spoke corridors, and distant sightlines toward Intake and Hangar.
- Optional objective: access a command terminal to reveal a partial record of the assassination order.
- Gate: operational routing opens Intake and Quarantine while locking hostile Grey access behind the player.
- Target time: 4 minutes.

### 08. Intake and Quarantine
- Depth: upper access level.
- Main mission: push through the Grey-controlled sanctioned entry system.
- Story beat: the Greys entered as official intervention forces, but their manipulated protocol now treats all witnesses as threats.
- Layout: decontamination lanes, security checkpoints, administrative rooms, and a high-risk quarantine bypass loop.
- Optional objective: disable an intervention beacon to weaken future Grey reinforcements.
- Gate: final security door opens after the quarantine control point is cleared.
- Target time: 4 minutes.

### 09. Strategic Command Vault
- Depth: upper command level adjacent to the hangar.
- Main mission: retrieve proof of the manipulated operation and unlock evacuation clearance.
- Story beat: the player confirms that an unknown human command layer arranged the assassination order, protocol change, and Aether seizure opportunity.
- Layout: secure data vault, tactical planning rooms, protected server chamber, and a short high-pressure escape corridor.
- Optional objective: copy hidden directives that become a sequel-facing lore collectible.
- Gate: the scientist uses recovered command clearance to unlock the hangar.
- Target time: 3 minutes.

### 10. Evacuation Hangar
- Depth: highest bunker exit level.
- Main mission: defeat the extraction guard and evacuate with the scientist and Aether sample.
- Story beat: the bunker is no longer a research site or covert battleground; it is a contested prize about to be erased or captured.
- Layout: broad aircraft bay, visible evacuation craft, cover lanes, open flanks, side maintenance catwalks, and a final extraction pad.
- Optional objective: save stranded staff or secure a final data crate before departure.
- Gate: the evacuation pad activates only after the guard is defeated.
- Target time: 4 minutes.

## Layout Principles
- Every level has a main route, one optional objective, a distinct landmark, and a story-justified gate.
- The route rises physically and politically: occult containment, industrial systems, research, operations, public-facing intake, then evacuation.
- Reptilian presence is strongest in the lower levels; Grey control becomes strongest in the upper levels.
- Aether recovery and scientist calibration create the central mid-act return/shortcut beat.
- Central Operations provides a visual reset and lets the player see the evacuation goal before the final push.
- No level relies on arbitrary locked doors. Each gate is tied to power, clearance, containment, calibration, routing, or evacuation security.

## Mapping Ownership
Ten levels are the campaign structure. For an eight-person mapping team, two people can own the two largest levels together:

1. Obsidian Briefing Vault and Containment Hive.
2. Geothermal Undercroft.
3. Cargo Logistics Depot.
4. Aether Relay Cathedral.
5. Bio-Research Laboratories.
6. Central Operations Nexus.
7. Intake and Quarantine.
8. Strategic Command Vault and Evacuation Hangar.

Shared entrances, elevators, shortcut borders, and global sightlines are reviewed as a group before any production blockout is accepted.

## Deferred Decisions

- Exact name and nature of the arriving hostile species.
- Identity of the hidden human command layer.
- Final enemy counts, enemy mixes, boss placements, and reward tables.
- Final art kit, lighting, audio, and HUD treatment.
